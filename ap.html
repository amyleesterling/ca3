An action potential, three ways

One mossy fiber and the CA3 pyramidal cell it contacts. Same event in all three, different camera.

The pulse is not a sweep across the screen. Every point on these meshes carries its own distance measured along the cell's skeleton, so the wavefront follows the real branching and splits wherever the dendrite splits. On the fiber it runs from the far end toward the boutons. In the cell it spreads outward from the 53 synapses themselves, which is why it leaves the thorn rather than the soma. The soma sits 130.5 µm of cable from the contacts, and that is what sets the moment it lights.
